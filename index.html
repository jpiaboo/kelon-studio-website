About

I've spent the last nine years moving renewable energy projects from feasibility study to commissioned asset — mostly solar microgrids for underserved health and agricultural facilities, and ESG roadmaps for manufacturers that need reduction targets they can actually hit.

I work at the point where climate ambition meets procurement, financing, and grid reality. My clients keep me around because the numbers I put in a proposal are the numbers that show up in the commissioning report.

Selected Work

Case studies

Three projects, three different constraints — a health system, a manufacturer, and a rural distribution network.

Microgrid
Solar Microgrid for Rural Health Access

Led a 500kW solar microgrid deployment powering three rural health clinics in Turkana County, replacing diesel generators that were failing cold-chain vaccine storage during outages.

62% cut in facility energy costs, first year
ESG Strategy
Scope 1–3 Emissions Roadmap

Advised a East African manufacturing client on a Scope 1–3 emissions reduction strategy — energy audits, supplier engagement, and a phased capital plan the board actually approved.

18% output reduction in 12 months
Energy Access
Community Clean Energy Access Program

Designed a pay-as-you-go solar financing model with local cooperatives, extending reliable power to households that had never had a grid connection.

12,000 households connected across 3 countries
